1. Executive Summary & Regulatory Problem

Automotive battery cell manufacturers and tier-1 cathode active material (CAM) producers face simultaneous statutory compliance deadlines in European and North American markets:

  • European Union — Regulation (EU) 2023/1542: Mandates a public-accessible Digital Battery Passport (Article 77) via an engraved QR code, life-cycle carbon footprint declarations (Article 7), and mandatory upstream supply-chain due diligence across cobalt, natural graphite, lithium, and nickel.
  • United States — Inflation Reduction Act (Public Law 117-169 § 30D): Mandates at least 50% (scaling to 80%) of critical minerals by procurement value originate from US or FTA partner nations, and strictly prohibits Foreign Entities of Concern (FEOC).

Traditional paper certificates and centralized databases cannot satisfy cross-border verification without exposing sensitive trade secrets or risking retrospective regulatory audits. This technical note specifies an onchain algorithmic oracle and EIP-712 cryptographic standard that mathematically certifies conformity.
